Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when using IntoDay.

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

IntoDay is the provider of the IntoDay app and related website. If you have questions about privacy or data handling, you can contact us at: contact.intoday@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve IntoDay.

Account Information: If you sign in with Google, we may receive basic account information such as your email address, your Google account identifier, and your name or profile image, if provided through your login permissions.

Content You Add to IntoDay: When you use IntoDay, we may store the content you choose to add, such as notes, links, files, references, and other materials you enter into the app to organize into reusable packs for your AI workflows.

Technical and Usage Data: When you use IntoDay, we may automatically collect certain technical information, such as device and browser information, IP address, log data, and usage analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and maintain IntoDay, enable secure login, store and display your notes and content, support syncing, monitor stability and security, and improve the app over time.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

We use trusted third-party service providers to operate IntoDay.

  • Supabase: We use Supabase for authentication, database, and backend-related services. Supabase may store your account data and the content you add to IntoDay as needed to provide the service.
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to log in with Google, Google provides the authentication process and may share basic account information with us.
  • Vercel: We use Vercel to host and deliver the IntoDay website and web app. Vercel may process technical information such as IP address and request logs for hosting and security purposes.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only with service providers that help us operate IntoDay, when required by law, or when necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of IntoDay and its users.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed to provide IntoDay and operate the service. If you delete your account or request deletion, we will make reasonable efforts to delete your personal data within a reasonable period, unless we are required to keep certain information for legal, security, or operational reasons.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data. To make a request, contact us at:
contact.intoday@gmail.com

9. Children's Privacy

IntoDay is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use such services without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

11. Contact

If you have any questions, feedback, or privacy-related requests, you can contact us at:
contact.intoday@gmail.com
